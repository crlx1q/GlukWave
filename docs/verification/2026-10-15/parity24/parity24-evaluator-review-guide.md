# GlukWave +24 independent visual review

Verdict: PASS. Public source and capture hashes are in parity24-evaluator-verdict.json. This was an independent agent using the same available provider; an external-provider evaluator was unavailable.

Fresh real-source app renders were inspected at 1440, 768 and 375 px in the isolated parity24-eval browser session. The original local fixture provided its own artwork, WAV clock and API data; no owner account was used. Actual Flutter widget trees were independently run for 390 px/full lyrics, 1440 px/full lyrics and six CD rotations, with 3/3 focused cases passing.

Observed outcomes:
- Footer 96 px at desktop and 72 px at tablet/phone, with zero attribution anchors; desktop lyrics sit in the metadata rail and responsive layouts hide that rail to preserve space.
- Actual media clock displayed 3 at 7.7 seconds, 2 at 8.8 seconds and 1 at 9.6 seconds, then the first timed line. Intro screenshots are parity24-eval-intro-{3,2,1}-375.png.
- Current/adjacent lyric lines remain sharp; farther lines have increasing subtle defocus. Phone/tablet active lines stay visible after resize.
- Web/native CD reverse and thickness surfaces remain opaque at 0, 82, 100, 180, 260 and 360 degrees.
- Final cover transition preserves initial artwork x/y/width through its layout commits, then moves once to the target; raw measured samples are parity24-eval-motion-final.json. Earlier motion traces are diagnostic and superseded.
- Pointer reflection reaches 20%/80% positions after the next animation frame with 0 s computed CSS transition duration.
- Native 1440 px full lyrics keep secondary controls visible; actual compact 144 px/125% text and remote footer 125% text renders were also inspected.
- Forced TV style render has a flat 270 px cover, 33 px clearance to controls, no lyric filters and a visible 2 px keyboard focus ring. Its dark fallback is documented by parity24-eval-tv-final-1440.png and parity24-eval-tv-focus-final-1440.png.

The complete final capture list is in the verdict JSON. Files prefixed parity24-eval-native- were captured by the independent native run. Other native 125% and excerpt captures were generated by the implementation agent's actual widget tests and visually inspected here.
